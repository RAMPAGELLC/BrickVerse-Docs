# Upload Limits and Restrictions

BrickVerse applies creation and upload limits to **Universes, Worlds, Entitlements, and Developer Assets**. These limits help keep the platform reliable, provide reasonable resource availability for creators, and reduce automated or abusive uploads.

Some limits are based on your current **Astro membership tier**. Higher Astro tiers include increased creation and monthly Developer Asset upload allowances.

This article explains the standard limits currently applied to BrickVerse accounts.

### Universe and World Creation Limits

BrickVerse experiences are organized into **Universes** and **Worlds**.

A **Universe** represents an experience or game on BrickVerse. Each Universe can contain one or more Worlds.

A **World** is an individual playable place within a Universe. Creators can use multiple Worlds to create separate maps, game modes, levels, lobbies, or other areas while keeping them together as part of the same experience.

The number of Universes you can create and the number of Worlds that can exist within each Universe depend on your Astro membership.

| Membership               | Maximum Universes | Worlds per Universe |
| ------------------------ | ----------------: | ------------------: |
| **User / No Membership** |                 2 |                   5 |
| **Astro Basic**          |                 5 |                   5 |
| **Astro**                |                20 |                  20 |
| **Astro Premium**        |                50 |                  25 |
| **Astro Studio**         |                50 |                  50 |

For example, a creator with **Astro** can create up to **20 Universes**, with each Universe containing up to **20 Worlds**.

These are creation limits rather than limits on how many people can play an experience. World server capacity, concurrent players, server instances, and other runtime limits are handled separately.

#### What happens when I reach my limit?

When your account reaches its Universe limit, you won't be able to create another Universe until capacity becomes available or your applicable limit increases.

Similarly, once a Universe reaches its World limit, additional Worlds cannot be created within that Universe.

Deleting content may free capacity where applicable. However, deletion should not be used as a method of repeatedly cycling content to circumvent platform restrictions or other applicable limits.

## Entitlement Limits

Each Universe can contain a maximum of **255 entitlements**.

This is a **per-Universe limit** and does not change based on your Astro membership.

| Membership           | Maximum Entitlements per Universe |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------: |
| User / No Membership |                               255 |
| Astro Basic          |                               255 |
| Astro                |                               255 |
| Astro Premium        |                               255 |
| Astro Studio         |                               255 |

If a Universe reaches 255 entitlements, additional entitlements cannot be created for that Universe unless existing entitlements are removed or the platform limit is changed.

The entitlement limit is separate from Universe, World, and Developer Asset limits.

## Developer Asset Upload Limits

BrickVerse allows creators to upload resources for use with their experiences through BrickVerse Creator and supported Creator services.

These are referred to as **Developer Assets**.

Developer Asset categories currently subject to these limits include:

* **Textures**
* **Sounds**
* **Meshes**
* **Prefabs**
* **Animations**
* **Plugins**

Developer Assets have **two different types of limits**:

**Monthly upload limits** determine how many new assets of each type an account may upload during a monthly period.

**Total asset limits** determine how many assets of each type an account may have in total.

These limits operate independently. Having monthly upload capacity remaining does not override your account's total asset limit.

***

## Monthly Developer Asset Upload Limits

Monthly Developer Asset allowances are determined by your **Astro membership**.

The limits apply **per user, per month, for each individual asset category**.

| Developer Asset | User / None | Astro Basic | Astro | Astro Premium | Astro Studio |
| --------------- | ----------: | ----------: | ----: | ------------: | -----------: |
| **Textures**    |          25 |          75 |   100 |           200 |          200 |
| **Sounds**      |           5 |          10 |    50 |           100 |          200 |
| **Meshes**      |           5 |          50 |   100 |           200 |          200 |
| **Prefabs**     |           5 |          25 |    50 |           100 |          200 |
| **Animations**  |          10 |          25 |    50 |           100 |          200 |
| **Plugins**     |           5 |          25 |    50 |           100 |          200 |

Each category has its own allowance.

For example, reaching your monthly **Mesh** upload limit does not prevent you from uploading **Textures** if you still have Texture uploads available.

#### User / No Membership

Creators do **not** need a paid Astro membership to upload Developer Assets.

Accounts without an Astro membership receive the standard monthly allowance of:

* 25 Textures
* 5 Sounds
* 5 Meshes
* 5 Prefabs
* 10 Animations
* 5 Plugins

This allows any BrickVerse creator to begin building and publishing content without requiring a paid membership.

#### Astro Basic

Astro Basic increases several limits, particularly for creators working with meshes and reusable Creator resources.

Each month, Astro Basic includes up to **75 Textures, 10 Sounds, 50 Meshes, 25 Prefabs, 25 Animations, and 25 Plugins**.

#### Astro

Astro provides substantially larger allowances for creators building more asset-intensive experiences.

Astro members can upload up to **100 Textures, 50 Sounds, 100 Meshes, 50 Prefabs, 50 Animations, and 50 Plugins** per month.

#### Astro Premium

Astro Premium is intended to provide considerably more capacity for active creators and larger projects.

Astro Premium allows up to **200 Textures, 100 Sounds, 200 Meshes, 100 Prefabs, 100 Animations, and 100 Plugins** per month.

#### Astro Studio

Astro Studio provides the highest standard monthly Developer Asset limits, allowing up to **200 uploads per month in every supported Developer Asset category**.

As noted above, **Astro Studio is not publicly sold**. It is reserved for BrickVerse partners and other creators or organizations specifically approved by BrickVerse.

***

## Total Developer Asset Limits

Monthly upload limits should not be confused with the maximum number of Developer Assets an account can have.

BrickVerse maintains the following default **per-user total asset limits**:

| Developer Asset | Maximum Assets |
| --------------- | -------------: |
| **Textures**    |            500 |
| **Sounds**      |            200 |
| **Meshes**      |            200 |
| **Prefabs**     |            100 |
| **Animations**  |            100 |
| **Plugins**     |            100 |

These limits apply to the total assets associated with the account rather than the number uploaded during a particular month.

#### Monthly limit vs. total limit

For example, suppose an **Astro Premium** member has already reached the default total limit of **200 Meshes**.

Although Astro Premium normally permits up to 200 Mesh uploads during the applicable monthly period, the creator cannot continue uploading additional Meshes after reaching the **200 total Mesh limit**.

In this situation, the total asset limit takes precedence.

Likewise, a creator with only 50 existing Meshes could still be prevented from uploading additional Meshes if they have already consumed their monthly Mesh upload allowance.

In short, **both limits must have available capacity for a new Developer Asset to be uploaded.**

***

## Requesting Increased Total Asset Limits

The total Developer Asset limits are the default maximums available to accounts.

Creators who legitimately require additional storage capacity may request a **manual limit increase from BrickVerse**.

Requests may be considered for established creators, larger projects, studios, BrickVerse partners, or other circumstances where the standard limits are insufficient.

Manual increases are reviewed by BrickVerse administrators and are **not automatically granted**.

BrickVerse may consider factors such as the purpose of the requested increase, the creator's existing projects, account standing, historical platform usage, moderation history, and the amount of additional capacity requested.

An approved increase may apply only to specific asset categories. For example, BrickVerse may increase an account's Mesh limit without changing its Texture or Sound limits.

***

## Asset Moderation and Upload Restrictions

Having available upload capacity does not guarantee that an asset will be accepted or permitted to remain on BrickVerse.

Developer Assets must comply with applicable **BrickVerse Terms of Service, Community Standards, Creator policies, intellectual-property requirements, and other platform rules**.

BrickVerse may reject, restrict, moderate, disable, or remove assets that violate platform policies.

An upload may also fail or be restricted for technical reasons, including unsupported file formats, invalid or corrupted files, file-size restrictions, processing failures, security restrictions, or other technical requirements imposed by BrickVerse Creator.

Upload limits represent the **maximum quantity of uploads permitted under normal circumstances** and do not override other technical or moderation requirements.

***

## Circumventing Upload Limits

Creators should not attempt to bypass or circumvent creation or asset limits.

This can include intentionally using alternate accounts, automated account creation, excessive duplicate uploads, or other methods designed primarily to avoid an account's applicable restrictions.

BrickVerse may restrict uploading, remove content, or take other appropriate action when platform systems are intentionally abused.

Legitimate creators who require limits beyond the standard allowances should instead contact BrickVerse and request a manual limit review.

***

## Membership Changes

Because several limits are based on Astro membership, changing membership tiers may change the limits available to your account.

Upgrading to a higher eligible Astro tier can provide increased Universe, World, and monthly Developer Asset capacity according to the tables above.

Your existing content is separate from the monthly upload allowance. A membership change does not itself delete previously uploaded Developer Assets, Worlds, or Universes.

However, your account remains subject to the limits and restrictions applicable to its current membership and to any separately assigned account limits.
